## The Numbers

- **1,178 total listings** across 274 cities (WA + OR)
- **758 (64%)** have a website (backlink-capable for beta barter)
- **1,147 (97%)** have a phone number
- **10 (<1%)** have an email address in our data
- **0** currently claimed by an external groomer

## Contact Channels (ranked by viability)

1. **Phone/text** - 97% coverage. Best for local, personal outreach.
2. **Website contact form** - 64% have sites. Find their contact page manually.
3. **Instagram/Facebook DM** - Most groomers post their work. Search "[business name] + city + grooming" on Instagram.
4. **In-person drop-in** - Highest conversion rate, lowest scale. Reserve for top-tier cities near you.
5. **Email** - <1% have emails in our data. Not viable without scraping.

## Outreach Phases

### Phase 1: Warm Start (April 15 - April 30)

**Goal:** 5-10 claimed listings. Prove the flow works. Get first testimonials.

**Focus:** Seattle + immediate surroundings (Tacoma, Bellevue, Puyallup, Gig Harbor)

**Method:** In-person drop-ins + phone follow-ups

**Script (in-person):**
> "Hi, I'm [name] from GroomLocal. We built a free grooming directory for the Pacific Northwest and your business is already listed. I wanted to stop by and let you know you can claim your profile, add your own photos, and get a Verified badge. Right now we're giving founding members all the premium features for free."

**Targeting within cities:**
1. Groomers with websites (backlink potential)
2. Groomers with active Instagram/social presence (likely to engage)
3. Groomers in the same neighborhood (visit multiple in one trip)

**Daily target:** Visit 3-5 groomers per day, 2-3 days per week

### Phase 2: Phone Blitz (May 1 - May 31)

**Goal:** 30-50 claimed listings across Tier 1 cities.

**Method:** Phone calls to all Tier 1 cities, starting with highest-count cities.

**Call script:**
> "Hi, this is [name] from GroomLocal. We're a new pet grooming directory for the Pacific Northwest. Your business is already listed at groomlocal.com, and I'm calling to let you know you can claim your profile for free. As a founding member, you'd get all our premium features at no cost, including a Verified badge, photo uploads, and priority placement. The only thing we ask is a link to GroomLocal on your website. Can I send you the link to claim your listing?"

**Batch schedule (by city size):**
- Week 1: Seattle (52), Portland (31) - highest volume
- Week 2: Tacoma (23), Vancouver (21), Spokane (18)
- Week 3: Bellingham, Salem, Kennewick, Bend, Eugene, Grants Pass
- Week 4: Remaining Tier 1 cities (Yakima through Walla Walla)

**Follow-up cadence:**
- Day 0: Initial call
- Day 3: Text if no answer ("Hi, this is [name] from GroomLocal...")
- Day 7: Second call attempt
- Day 14: Final text with direct claim link

### Phase 3: Expand to Tier 2 (June - July)

**Goal:** 100+ claimed listings. Start showing social proof on city pages.

**Method:** Phone + text to Tier 2 cities. Prioritize cities with 70%+ website rates (higher backlink yield).

**New angle (once you have testimonials):**
> "Over 30 groomers in the PNW have already claimed their listing, including [name] in [nearby city]. Here's what they said: '[testimonial quote]'"

### Phase 4: Scale Outreach (August+)

- Tier 3 cities via batch text campaigns
- Tier 4 organic only (let SEO drive discovery)
- Start automated email sequences for newly listed businesses
- Consider hiring a VA for phone outreach at $15-20/hr
