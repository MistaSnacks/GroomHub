# Maui Base Prompt (Reproducible)

This is the prompt source used by `scripts/maui-blog-image.sh`. It is intentionally short, structured, and free of contradictions. It is derived from the `reproducible_maui_prompt` template that has consistently produced on-brand Maui across the existing asset library.

For human reference (scene table, style notes, hind-legs convention), see `MAUI-PROMPT-GUIDE.md`. The script no longer pulls from that file.

MAUI BASE PROMPT:
---
CHARACTER BASE: A small, fluffy white/cream-colored dog (Maltese/Bichon Frise mix). Kawaii proportions with a large round head relative to a compact body. Big round dark brown eyes, small round black nose, open smiling mouth showing a pink tongue. Soft fluffy fur with subtle warm beige shading.

ANATOMY REQUIREMENTS: Exactly 4 legs TOTAL (2 front, 2 back). Normal dog anatomy. No extra limbs.

SIGNATURE ACCESSORY: Always wearing a red bandana tied around the neck with the word "MAUI" in white text and a small white paw print on it.

COMPOSITION & ART STYLE: Hand-drawn cartoon illustration matching exactly to the base reference. Extremely thick, clean black outlines on the exterior silhouette. Thinner clean lines for interior details. Flat cel-shaded coloring with soft subtle shading, no gradients. Sticker-like quality. Pure white (#FFFFFF) background, no shadows behind the character. NOT 3D, NOT pixel art.
---
