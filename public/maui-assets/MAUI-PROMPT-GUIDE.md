# Maui Mascot - Image Generation Prompt Guide

## Base Character Prompt (use this as the foundation for every Maui image)

```
MAUI BASE PROMPT:
---
Character: A small, fluffy white/cream-colored dog (Maltese/Bichon Frise mix). Kawaii proportions with a large round head relative to a compact body. Big round dark brown eyes, small round black nose, open smiling mouth showing a pink tongue. Soft fluffy fur with subtle warm beige shading. Floppy ears that blend into the fluffy head fur. Short fluffy tail, curled up. Small round paws.

Signature accessory: Always wearing a red bandana tied around the neck with the word "MAUI" in white text and a small white paw print on it. The bandana is the character's key identifier and must always be visible.

Art style: Hand-drawn cartoon illustration. Clean black outlines (thicker on the exterior silhouette, thinner on interior details). Flat cel-shaded coloring with subtle soft shading, no gradients. Sticker-like quality with clean edges. NOT pixel art, NOT 3D render, NOT realistic. Kawaii influence. Friendly, warm, and approachable.

Composition: Character centered in frame. Square format. Pure white (#FFFFFF) or transparent background, no color, no gradients, no shadows behind the character. No complex backgrounds. Small contextual props relevant to the scene placed around the character.

Mood: Happy, friendly, cheerful.
---
```

## How to Use

1. Start with the full base prompt above
2. Add a SCENE line describing what Maui is doing and what props are around him
3. Add a MOOD line if the mood differs from the default cheerful tone
4. Keep props simple and iconic (one or two items, not complex scenes)

## Scene Prompt Template

```
[FULL BASE PROMPT FROM ABOVE]

Scene: Maui is [DOING WHAT] with [WHAT PROPS] around him.
Mood: [cheerful / curious / cozy / gentle / playful / etc.]
```

## Rules

- The red "MAUI" bandana must ALWAYS be visible, even if Maui is wearing a costume or accessory
- Keep backgrounds clean (white or transparent), no busy scenes
- Props should be simple, iconic, and immediately recognizable at small sizes
- Maui should always look happy and friendly (exceptions: curious expression for investigative scenes)
- One or two props max per scene to keep the sticker-like quality
- No text in the image other than "MAUI" on the bandana
