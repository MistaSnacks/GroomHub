# Project instructions

## Maui artwork

Before creating, editing, or choosing any Maui illustration, read
`docs/maui/STYLE-STANDARD.md` and `public/maui-assets/MAUI-BASE-PROMPT.md`.
The six files under `docs/maui/approved-reference/` are the owner's explicit approved
appearance standard. Use `brushing-v2.png` as the default visual reference for every
new scene. Preserve Maui's face, proportions, burgundy outlines, cream/tan fur, and red
MAUI bandana. Older assets and archived comparisons must not redefine his appearance.
Keep these fixed reference masters intact and verify complete anatomy and unclipped edges.

All site-facing Maui illustrations require real RGBA transparency. Preserve approved opaque
masters, remove only the background, and verify on teal/dark before installing. Follow the
background-removal section of the style standard; never deploy a cream rectangle or fake checks.
