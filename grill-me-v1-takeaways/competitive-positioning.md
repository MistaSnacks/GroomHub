# Competitive Positioning

## The Big Players
- Yelp: $300+/mo for ads, buries non-paying businesses, owns your reviews
- Google Maps/Business: Free but no differentiation, no community
- Thumbtack: Lead-based pricing, expensive per lead
- Nextdoor: Noisy, not grooming-specific

## GroomLocal's Advantages

### 1. Built by a Groomer
- Owner runs an actual grooming business in the PNW
- Understands the pain points firsthand
- Can speak the language, not a tech company selling to groomers

### 2. In-Person Local Outreach
- Can physically visit groomers in the PNW to pitch
- Personal relationship building that no big platform will ever do
- "The little guy" positioning against Yelp resonates with small business owners
- Start with Seattle/Portland metro, expand outward

### 3. Local Pack Opportunity
- PNW-focused directory can build local domain authority
- Embedded in local communities (pet events, groomer meetups, partnerships)
- Hyperlocal content (neighborhoods, dog parks, trails) that national platforms don't invest in

### 4. CRM Integration (Future)
- Not just a listing, but a business tool
- "List your business AND manage your clients" is a stronger pitch than just a directory
- No competitor offers directory + CRM as a bundle for groomers

### 5. Price
- Fraction of Yelp's cost
- Transparent pricing, no pay-to-play review manipulation

## The Pitch (Draft)
"I'm [name], I run a grooming business here in [city]. I built GroomLocal because I was tired of paying Yelp $300 a month to show up in searches. GroomLocal is a directory built by groomers, for groomers. Your listing is already up, you can claim it for free and start getting found. And if you want priority placement and leads, it's a fraction of what Yelp charges. We're also building a CRM so you can manage your clients in one place."

## Outreach Plan
- [ ] Claim own business first (dogfood the experience)
- [ ] Visit 10-20 groomers in your metro area in person
- [ ] Bring a one-pager or business card with QR code to their listing
- [ ] Offer 3 months free on paid tier for early adopters
- [ ] Collect feedback on what features matter most
- [ ] Use their feedback to prioritize development
