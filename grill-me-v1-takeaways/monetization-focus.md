# Monetization: Four Streams, Need to Prioritize

## Current Streams (all pre-revenue)
1. **Ad revenue** - AdSlot components exist as placeholders, no ad network integrated
2. **Own business top placement** - Not yet listed, needs directory to rank on Google first
3. **CRM product** - Separate product, built but not integrated with GroomLocal
4. **Lead gen / subscriptions** - Pricing tiers built, claims flow exists, zero paying customers

## The Problem
Four streams = four times the work with zero focus. Each one requires different infrastructure, different audiences, and different go-to-market motions.

## Recommended Priority Order

### Phase 1: SEO Traffic (no revenue, but enables everything else)
- Get GroomLocal ranking for PNW grooming searches
- Claim own business as showcase listing
- This is the foundation. Without traffic, ads pay nothing, leads don't exist, and no groomer will pay for a listing

### Phase 2: Own Business Benefit (indirect revenue)
- Once ranking, your own grooming business gets free leads from top placement
- This is the easiest "revenue" because it's just cost savings on marketing for your existing business
- Also validates that the directory actually drives business

### Phase 3: Subscriptions / Lead Gen (direct revenue)
- Once you can prove "I got X leads from GroomLocal" with your own business, that's your sales pitch
- Start outreach to other groomers with data
- Keep tiers simple, maybe even just Free and one Paid tier to start

### Phase 4: Ad Revenue (scale revenue)
- Only worth pursuing after significant traffic (50k+ monthly visits minimum for meaningful ad income)
- Can layer in once the directory has organic growth

### CRM: Separate Track
- Don't try to integrate the CRM into GroomLocal yet
- It can be an upsell offered during outreach ("We also have a CRM that pairs with your listing")
- Integration (leads from GroomLocal flowing into CRM) is a Phase 3+ feature

## Key Insight
The directory's first job is to rank on Google and drive traffic to YOUR business. That's the proof of concept. Everything else comes after.
