# Homepage: Add Groomer CTA to Hero

## Decision
Add a secondary CTA in the homepage hero targeting groomers. The primary CTA stays pet-owner focused (search/browse). The secondary is a smaller, less prominent link for groomers.

## Why
- Groomers who land on the homepage organically need a clear path to claim
- Pet owners are the primary audience so the groomer CTA should not compete visually
- During outreach, direct links to the groomer's listing are the main flow, but the homepage should still catch groomers who arrive on their own

## Implementation
- Small text link or secondary button below the main hero CTA
- Something like: "Are you a groomer? Claim your free listing" linking to a search or claim info page
- Keep it subtle - secondary text size, muted color, not a full button competing with the primary CTA

## Action Items
- [ ] Add secondary groomer CTA to HomeHero component
- [ ] Link to appropriate destination (claim info page or search with groomer context)
- [ ] Keep visual hierarchy: pet owner CTA primary, groomer CTA secondary
