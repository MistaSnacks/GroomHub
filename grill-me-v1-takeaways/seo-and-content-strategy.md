# SEO and Content Strategy

## Current State (as of March 2026)
- Site live for ~2 weeks, no meaningful rankings yet (normal, expect 4-8 weeks minimum)
- Sitemap submitted to Google Search Console
- DNS recently moved to Vercel (no SEO impact)
- 1,177 listings, 50+ city pages, 7 blog posts, 15 service pages, 8 specialty pages

## The Ranking Reality
- Individual groomer pages won't outrank Yelp/Google Business anytime soon
- City pages with enriched content (neighborhoods, dog parks, trails, FAQs) are the strongest SEO asset
- Blog posts targeting informational queries are a strong differentiator
- Domain authority takes 3-6 months to build

## Content Priorities (in order of impact)

### 1. City Page Content (highest SEO value)
- [ ] Ensure every city page has unique neighborhood guides, not just listing grids
- [ ] Add local dog park and trail content for remaining cities
- [ ] Write unique FAQs per city (not templated across all cities)
- [ ] Target "dog grooming [city name]" keywords naturally in content
- [ ] Add seasonal/weather-specific grooming tips per region

### 2. Blog Posts (informational keyword capture)
- [ ] Continue publishing SEO-forward posts (pricing, seasonal, how-to guides)
- [ ] Target questions people ask before booking: "how often should I groom my dog", "what to expect at first grooming", "grooming for anxious dogs"
- [ ] Aim for 2-4 posts per month
- [ ] Interlink blog posts to relevant city and service pages

### 3. Service and Specialty Pages
- [ ] Add more descriptive content to each service page (not just a filtered listing grid)
- [ ] Answer "what is [service]" and "who needs [service]" on each page
- [ ] These target mid-funnel queries like "deshedding grooming near me"

### 4. Groomer Profile Enrichment
- [ ] Encourage claimed groomers to fill out descriptions, photos, hours
- [ ] Richer profiles = more unique content per page = better indexing
- [ ] This happens naturally as groomers claim and edit, not something you can force

## Search Console Checklist
- [ ] Verify property under new Vercel DNS (re-add TXT record if needed)
- [ ] Check coverage report for indexing errors
- [ ] Monitor "Pages" report for crawl issues
- [ ] Check that sitemap shows all URLs as discovered/indexed over time
- [ ] Set up weekly check-in habit (every Monday, 5 minutes)

## What NOT To Do
- Don't obsess over rankings in the first 3 months
- Don't create thin pages just to target keywords (Google penalizes this)
- Don't duplicate content across city pages with find-and-replace city names
- Don't buy backlinks or do link schemes

## Backlink Strategy (low effort, high value)
- [ ] List GroomLocal on PNW pet directories and local business roundups
- [ ] Reach out to local pet bloggers for mentions
- [ ] Submit to "best of" lists for pet services in Seattle/Portland
- [ ] Your own grooming business website should link to GroomLocal
- [ ] Any groomer who claims their listing should be encouraged to link back from their site
