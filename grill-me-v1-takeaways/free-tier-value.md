# Free Tier: Needs More Value

## Current State
After claiming, a free groomer can:
- Edit name, description, phone, email
- Upload 1 photo
- Manage account

That's it. Hours and website are gated behind Standard ($15/mo) which has no payment processing yet.

## The Problem
If claiming feels pointless, groomers won't do it. And if they don't claim, they'll never upgrade. The free tier is the top of the funnel. It needs to feel generous.

## Recommendation: Give More Away for Free
Move these INTO the free tier:
- [ ] Website link (this is basic business info, not a premium feature)
- [ ] Business hours (same, this is expected on any directory)
- [ ] 3 photos instead of 1 (1 feels insulting, 3 is enough to showcase)
- [ ] Basic view count ("Your listing was viewed X times this month")

This makes claiming feel worthwhile. The paid tier then sells:
- Unlimited photos
- Priority placement
- Badges
- Lead gen / contact form
- Detailed analytics
- Ad removal

## Bug Found
The edit page shows the website input field for all tiers, but the pricing page says it's Standard+. Either gate it properly or (recommended) just make it free.

## Key Principle
Free tier = generous enough to hook them. Paid tier = powerful enough to justify the cost. Right now free is too stingy and paid doesn't exist.
