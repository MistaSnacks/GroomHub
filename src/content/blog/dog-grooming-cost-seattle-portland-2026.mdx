Dog grooming prices in Seattle and Portland depend on the salon, your dog's size and coat, and whether the appointment includes a haircut. **Published full-groom examples in the local menus below start around $80 for a small dog and reach $220 or more for larger dogs.** Mobile appointments have separate pricing. These examples help with budgeting; they are not a citywide average or a quote for your dog.

We checked the linked providers' public menus on September 7, 2026. Ask for a current estimate before booking, especially if your dog has a curly coat, matting, or needs extra handling time.

[Seattle Canine Club's grooming menu](https://seattlecanineclub.com/services/dog-grooming-seattle/) lists the following long-coat full-groom prices:

| 11–20 lb | $120 |
| 21–30 lb | $135 |
| 31–40 lb | $155 |
| 41–55 lb | $175 |
| 56–80 lb | $190 |

This is one provider's menu, not an average for Seattle. Its site also describes location-specific pricing and additional charges for some coats or services. Confirm the location, package, and final quote directly.

### Bath, tidy, or full groom?

A bath, a tidy, and a full haircut are different services. At PDX Pet Spa, the small-dog basic bath is listed at $35, while a small-dog full haircut is $80–$90. That gap is why an advertised bath price should not be used as a haircut estimate.

Ask whether a quote includes drying, brushing, face/feet/sanitary trimming, a full-body haircut, and nail care. Names such as “basic,” “full service,” and “spa package” are not standardized across salons.

## Salon vs. Mobile vs. Chain Grooming

Compare the package and final estimate rather than assuming that a business format guarantees a lower price or better service.

- **Independent salon:** Check what is included, how appointments are scheduled, and whether your dog will spend time waiting between stages.
- **Chain salon:** Obtain a quote for the specific location, breed, and service. Don't rely on one nationwide starting price as the cost of your dog's haircut.
- **Mobile groomer:** Confirm the service area, suitable parking, package, and any minimum visit charge. The workspace comes to you, but prices vary widely.

For mobile examples, [Full Moon Groom in SW Portland](https://www.fullmoongroom.com/) lists small, medium, and large full grooms from $145, $175, and $220 respectively. [Girl & Doggies in the Seattle area](https://girldoggies.com/) lists full grooms at $160 small, $220 medium, $270 large, and $300 extra-large. Size definitions and package contents differ, so these are examples rather than a calculated mobile surcharge.

## Add-Ons and Extra Charges

Ask for the provider's actual extra-service menu. As examples, The Groom Shop lists extra brushing from $15, deshedding from $25, nail trims from $25, and teeth brushing from $15. These are that salon's published prices, not standard fees across the region.

Before agreeing to a groom, ask:

1. What is the estimated total for the requested haircut?
2. How much brushing or coat preparation is included?
3. What happens if the groomer finds matting or needs more time?
4. Will they contact you before work exceeds the estimate?
5. Are taxes, gratuity, late pickup, or cancellation charges separate?

## Why Can a Grooming Appointment Cost So Much?

The quote reflects the work involved, not just the dog's weight. Bathing, drying, brushing, cutting, and finishing all take time. A long curly coat or a complicated style can require more work than a short coat on a similarly sized dog. Extra handling time can also affect what a provider is able to offer.

Ask how the business prices your dog's coat and requested cut. Avoid choosing a longer style solely from a photo without discussing the maintenance it needs. Read our [guide to choosing a groomer](/blog/choosing-the-right-groomer-for-your-pet) for other questions to ask.

## How Much to Tip Your Dog Groomer

Check whether gratuity is already included. If you choose to tip 15–20%, these examples show the arithmetic:

| Service price | 15% tip | 20% tip |
|---|---|---|
| $70 | $10.50 | $14 |
| $100 | $15 | $20 |
| $150 | $22.50 | $30 |
| $200 | $30 | $40 |

A tip is discretionary; these amounts are not required fees. Budget for the agreed service first, and ask about a salon's tipping policy if it is unclear.

## Plan a Grooming Budget

Use a quote and an interval suited to your dog's coat. For example, a $120 appointment every six weeks annualizes to about 8.7 visits, or $1,040 before tips and extra services. Actual calendar bookings are whole visits; nine appointments would cost $1,080. More frequent appointments or a higher quote will change that total.

Ask the groomer to show you how to maintain the coat between visits and choose a haircut you can realistically look after. Our [grooming frequency guide](/blog/how-often-should-you-groom-your-dog) can help you discuss a suitable routine.

Compare [PNW groomers](/dog-grooming), or use the [quote request form](/get-quotes) with your dog's weight, coat type, desired haircut, and date of the last groom. Confirm prices directly with the business before treating them as final.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the average price to have a dog groomed in Seattle or Portland?

We do not have a representative citywide average. The linked menus give concrete examples: small-dog full haircuts at the two Portland salons start at $80 or $85, while Seattle Canine Club's long-coat menu starts at $100 for its smallest bracket. Larger dogs and additional services can cost substantially more.

### How much does it cost to groom a 30 lb dog?

Seattle Canine Club lists $135 for its 21–30 lb long-coat full groom. PDX Pet Spa lists $110–$130 for a full haircut in its 25–50 lb bracket. These are provider examples; confirm how your dog's coat and style affect the quote.

### How much do you tip on a $70 dog groom?

If you choose 15%, the tip is $10.50; at 20%, it is $14. Check whether gratuity is already included. The amount is your choice, rather than an automatic extra charge.

### Does a full groom include nail trimming?

The menus cited here include nail care in their full-groom packages, but you should confirm the exact service. Clipping and grinding can be priced differently, and a nail-only visit may have its own fee.

### Is mobile grooming always more expensive?

No fixed premium applies to every pair of providers. Compare the same dog's haircut and included services, plus any travel or extra-work charges. Our [mobile pricing guide](/blog/mobile-dog-grooming-cost-guide) has current local menu examples.
