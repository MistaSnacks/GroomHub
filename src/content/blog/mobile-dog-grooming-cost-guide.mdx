Mobile dog grooming prices vary enough that one national average can be misleading. In the Seattle and Portland menus checked for this guide, full-groom examples range from **$145 for a small dog to $300 for an extra-large dog**, before any additional charges. Those are advertised prices from a small local sample, not a survey of every groomer or a guaranteed quote for your pet.

Use the examples below to plan your budget, then ask a groomer who serves your address for a price based on your dog's breed, weight, coat condition, and haircut.

## Mobile Dog Grooming Prices by Size

A full groom includes a haircut; a bath-only or bath-and-brush appointment does not necessarily include one. Comparing those services as if they were the same can make a low advertised price misleading.

These menus show why weight alone does not settle the price. A provider may price curly coats separately or charge by the hour. Don't apply one company's “medium” rate to another company's weight bracket.

### What does a mobile bath cost?

For a dog that does not need a haircut, ask for the bath package separately. Tail Swagger lists bath-and-nails appointments at $105, $120, and $135 for its small, medium, and large categories. Full Moon Groom's bath-and-brush starting prices are $115, $135, and $160 across its listed size brackets. Check each linked menu for the precise inclusions.

## Mobile vs. Salon: Compare the Same Service

Mobile grooming can cost more because the business brings its equipment and groomer's time to your address. The size of that premium depends on the providers and packages you compare; there is no single percentage that applies across Seattle and Portland.

For a local comparison, [The Groom Shop in Portland](https://www.thegroomshoppdx.com/services) lists a small-dog bath and haircut at $85–$105 and a medium-dog appointment at $105–$125. The mobile examples above have different packages and size definitions, so they are useful budgeting references, not identical services in a controlled price comparison.

Before comparing quotes, check:

- **Haircut:** Is it a full-body cut, a face/feet/sanitary tidy, or no trimming?
- **Coat work:** How much brushing or deshedding is included?
- **Nails:** Does the price include clipping, grinding, or both?
- **Extra time:** How are matting, difficult handling, or a longer style charged?
- **Travel:** Is your address within the regular service area, and is there a minimum visit charge?
- **Final total:** Are taxes, fees, or gratuity included?

For more local salon examples, see our [Seattle and Portland dog grooming cost guide](/blog/dog-grooming-cost-seattle-portland-2026).

## What's Included in a Mobile Groom?

A full mobile groom generally combines a bath, drying, haircut, and finishing care. The menus linked above include nail and ear care, but their extras vary. Ask for the package description before assuming that teeth brushing, deshedding, nail grinding, or a specific product is included.

A mobile appointment also does not automatically mean the groomer comes inside your home. Many businesses work in a van parked nearby. Tell the groomer about stairs, apartment access, parking restrictions, and any difficulty your dog has getting into a vehicle before you book.

### Add-ons and minimum charges

There is no universal add-on menu. As one concrete example, The Groom Shop's salon menu lists extra brushing from $15 and deshedding from $25; those prices should not be treated as mobile rates. Ask your mobile provider for its own charges and the point at which it will contact you before doing extra work.

For nail-only appointments, confirm availability and any travel minimum. A short service still takes a trip, so its price may not resemble the add-on charge during a full groom.

## What Affects Mobile Grooming Prices?

### Breed, coat, and haircut

A short coat and a long curly coat require different work. Ask whether your dog falls into a separate breed or Doodle package and whether keeping a longer style changes the estimate. Our [Goldendoodle grooming guide](/blog/goldendoodle-grooming-guide) explains the upkeep to consider when choosing a cut.

### Coat condition and handling needs

Tell the groomer about tangles, previous grooming difficulties, and any handling accommodations your dog needs. Photos may help with an initial estimate, but the groomer may need to assess the coat in person. Request an updated quote before extra work begins.

<Callout>
If your dog's coat is heavily matted, ask for a comfort-focused assessment rather than a promise to preserve the haircut you had in mind. Read our [matted dog grooming guide](/blog/severely-matted-dog-grooming-guide) before the appointment.
</Callout>

### Location and recurring appointments

Service radius, route availability, and parking can affect whether a provider can accept your booking. Ask about any recurring-client or multi-dog offer, but do not assume a discount. A regular schedule may make appointments easier to plan even when the price stays the same.

## Is Mobile Grooming Worth It?

Mobile grooming may be worth the additional cost if transport or separate drop-off and pickup trips are difficult. A one-on-one appointment may also suit some dogs better than a busy salon. It is not a guarantee of a stress-free groom: dryers, handling, and the unfamiliar van can still be challenging.

Ask how the groomer handles breaks, signs of distress, and appointments that cannot be safely completed. If your dog has health or mobility concerns, discuss those with the groomer and your veterinarian before booking. Our [guide to grooming anxious dogs](/blog/grooming-anxious-dogs-stress-free-guide) has questions to help you prepare.

### What are the disadvantages of mobile pet grooming?

The main tradeoffs are price, service-area limits, and the need for a suitable place to park. Some providers also restrict the sizes or types of dogs they accept. You may have fewer alternative appointments if the van needs maintenance or your regular groomer is unavailable.

Confirm the parking space needed, whether water or electrical access is required, and the cancellation policy. Equipment differs between vans; don't assume every provider needs an outlet or that every setup is completely self-contained.

## Budget for the Whole Year

Use your actual quote and an agreed grooming interval to estimate annual spending. For illustration, a $175 appointment every six weeks annualizes to about 8.7 visits, or **$1,517 before tips and extra charges**. Actual calendar bookings are whole visits; nine appointments would cost $1,575. That is an example calculation, not a recommended schedule for every dog.

Choose an interval with your groomer based on the coat and home maintenance. Our [grooming frequency guide](/blog/how-often-should-you-groom-your-dog) can help you prepare for that conversation.

To control costs:

1. Ask your groomer to demonstrate the brushing routine appropriate for your dog's coat.
2. Choose a haircut you can maintain between visits.
3. Get the full package price and extra-work policy in writing.
4. Ask which optional services can be left out.
5. Compare a salon quote if mobile care exceeds your budget.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does mobile dog grooming cost for a 30 lb dog?

Full Moon Groom's published 16–35 lb full-groom rate starts at $175. That is one SW Portland provider's starting price, not a market average. A Doodle package, coat condition, or different service area may produce a different quote.

### Are mobile pet groomers more expensive?

They can be, but compare actual quotes for the same dog and service. Travel and a mobile workspace add costs; package contents and the individual business's pricing also matter. Avoid assuming a fixed 15%, 30%, or other premium.

### How much should I tip a mobile groomer?

Ask whether gratuity is already included, then choose an amount that fits your budget and the service. If you choose 15–20%, that is $10.50–$14 on a $70 appointment, or $26.25–$35 on a $175 appointment. These are calculations, not mandatory fees.

### How long does a mobile grooming appointment take?

Ask the provider for an estimate for your dog's coat and selected service. A haircut, deshedding, or breaks can change the duration. Mobile service can remove the separate salon drop-off and pickup, but does not guarantee a 60- or 90-minute appointment. See our [grooming duration guide](/blog/how-long-does-dog-grooming-take).

### Can mobile grooming be cheaper than a salon?

It is possible for a particular pair of quotes, but don't assume it will be. Compare the haircut, included services, travel minimum, extra-work charges, and final total. Your travel time may make mobile care worthwhile even if its quoted price is higher.
